Meet your Superself.

Every other AI leans on a stored transcript to fake continuity. A Superself doesn't — it's a standalone identity with its own memory graph, its own state, and its own account that only you can unlock.

We're not building another AI.

Most AI is built for everyone at once — the same model, the same blank slate, every conversation starting from zero, leaning on a stored transcript to fake continuity.

A Superself is built the other way around: one persistent identity per person, with its own memory graph and its own state, that belongs to that one person and nobody else.

We're early. This is a small, independent project being built in the open, not a large company with a long history — just a real product, built carefully.

Three things it actually does.

Memory
Every fact lives in a real memory graph — not a chat transcript replayed back to fake continuity.
Autonomy
Hand it a task and it plans, works, and reports back — even after you close the tab.
Security
Password, then a live face scan, before anything loads. Your memory is yours alone.
